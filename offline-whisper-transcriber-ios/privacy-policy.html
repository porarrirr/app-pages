Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

This policy explains how the iOS and iPadOS app Whisper: Offline Transcription (also shown as "Whisper Transcriber" or "Whisper文字起こし"; the "App") handles information. It is provided by an individual developer, porarrirr ("we"). This policy is not legal advice and does not relieve you of your own legal obligations, such as getting consent before recording others. See also the Disclaimer.

In short: Whisper transcription runs on your device. The App has no ads, no analytics SDKs and no third-party tracking, and it does not send your audio or transcripts to us. The App connects to the internet to download model files and to check for updates. An optional Apple Intelligence setting, off by default, sends transcript text to Apple's Private Cloud Compute.

1. Information processed on your device

The App processes the following information locally. It is stored in the App's storage on your device.

DataPurposeWhere it is kept
Microphone recordingsTranscription and playbackOn your device (you can delete them)
Imported audio and videoTranscriptionThe file you select; a working copy may be kept on your device
Videos picked from PhotosTranscriptionOnly the video you pick is read
Transcripts, titles, tags, favoritesHistory, search, editing and exportOn your device
Apple Intelligence chatsShowing your chat history for a transcriptOn your device
Settings (model, language, options)Providing the App's featuresOn your device
Diagnostic logsTroubleshootingOn your device; shared only if you choose "Share Logs"

Depending on your iOS settings, App data may be included in your iCloud or computer backups, which are handled by Apple and by you.

2. Network connections

The App connects to the internet in these cases:

  • Model downloads (Hugging Face). Whisper models, the VAD (silence detection) model and Core ML encoders are downloaded over HTTPS from Hugging Face when you choose to download them. Hugging Face may log technical information such as your IP address under its own policies. No audio or transcripts are sent.
  • Update check (Apple). The App may ask the Apple App Store lookup service whether a newer version is available, using the App's bundle identifier. No audio or transcripts are sent.
  • Apple's speech engine (iOS 26 or later). If you use Apple's on-device speech engine or Live Transcribe, iOS may download and manage speech models for your language. This is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy.
  • Private Cloud Compute (optional, iOS 27 or later). This setting is off by default. If you turn it on, the transcript text (or parts of it) and your question are sent to Apple's Private Cloud Compute to generate answers and titles. Apple processes this data under its own privacy terms. When the setting is off, Apple Intelligence features in the App use the on-device model.

Whisper transcription itself runs on your device. After a model is downloaded, it is designed to work without an internet connection.

3. Ads, analytics and tracking

  • The App does not show ads.
  • The App does not use analytics or advertising SDKs.
  • The App does not track you across apps or websites, and does not request App Tracking Transparency permission.
  • The App does not require an account.

Apple may provide us with aggregated, anonymized statistics (such as download counts or crash reports from users who opt in) through App Store Connect. These do not include your recordings or transcripts.

4. Sharing

We do not receive, sell or share your audio, transcripts or chats. Data leaves your device only when you choose to share it — for example, by exporting a transcript, sharing a recording or logs through the iOS share sheet — or through the optional Private Cloud Compute setting described above. Where shared content goes after that depends on the destination you choose.

5. Permissions

  • Microphone: used only to record audio for transcription.
  • Speech recognition: used for Apple's on-device speech engine and Live Transcribe, where available.
  • Photos: the system picker gives the App access only to the video you select.

You can change permissions at any time in the iOS Settings app.

6. Deleting your data

  • Transcripts and recordings: delete them from History in the App.
  • Chats: delete individual chats from a transcript's chat list.
  • Tags: delete them from Manage Tags.
  • Models: delete them in Settings.
  • All App data: deleting the App removes its data from your device. Copies in backups or in places you shared to are not affected.

7. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example when features change. The latest version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date. Significant changes may also be mentioned in the App's release notes.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through GitHub Issues. Issues are public, so please don't include personal information. See Support for details.
