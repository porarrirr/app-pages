Help & support

Setup steps, tips and answers to common problems with Whisper: Offline Transcription for iPhone and iPad.

On this page
  1. Requirements
  2. First-time setup
  3. Tips for better results
  4. Troubleshooting
  5. Managing storage & data
  6. Contact

Requirements

  • iOS / iPadOS 17.0 or later on iPhone or iPad.
  • iOS 26 or later for Live Transcribe, Apple's on-device speech engine and on-device Apple Intelligence chat (on supported devices).
  • iOS 27 or later for the optional Private Cloud Compute mode.
  • An internet connection and free storage to download a model (about 15 MB to 1.5 GB depending on the model).
  • Microphone permission for recording.

First-time setup

  1. Open the app. A model download screen appears.
  2. Download a Whisper model. Base (about 142 MB) is the default and a reasonable starting point.
  3. When the download finishes, tap the record button or choose Select File / Select Video from Photos to start.
  4. Optional: in Settings, you can download the VAD model (to skip silent sections) or a Core ML encoder (which may speed up processing on supported devices).

After a model is downloaded, transcription with that model can usually run without an internet connection.

Tips for better results

  • Set the language in Settings if auto-detection picks the wrong one.
  • Try a larger model (Small, Medium or Large v3 Turbo) if words are often missed.
  • Add vocabulary hints for names, product names or technical terms that come up often.
  • Turn on audio cleanup for noisy recordings, and Skip Silent Sections for recordings with long pauses.
  • Keep the microphone close to the speaker and reduce background noise where you can.

Troubleshooting

The model download fails or stops

Check your network connection and make sure there is enough free storage for the model. You can retry or choose a different model from Settings → Model & Downloads. Large models may take a while on slower connections.

The microphone doesn't work

Open the iOS Settings app → Privacy & Security → Microphone and make sure the app is allowed. If another app is using the microphone, close it and try again. You can also pick a different input with the microphone switcher.

Transcription is slow, or the app runs out of memory

Try a smaller model (Tiny, Base or a Q5 variant) and turn on Flash Attention in the advanced settings. Very long files need more memory and time; splitting a long recording into shorter parts may help.

The result is in the wrong language or contains strange text

Choose the spoken language in Settings instead of Auto-Detect. Make sure "Translate to English" is off unless you want an English translation. Long silences can sometimes produce repeated phrases; turning on Skip Silent Sections may reduce this.

Live Transcribe or Apple's speech engine isn't available

These features need iOS 26 or later, and the language model for your language may need to be prepared first. Open Settings in the app to manage the iOS speech models.

Apple Intelligence chat isn't available

Chat needs iOS 26 or later on a device that supports Apple Intelligence, with Apple Intelligence turned on in the iOS Settings app. Availability may also depend on region and language.

An imported video or audio file can't be transcribed

Some files may use formats or protection that iOS can't read. Try exporting the file again as M4A, MP3, WAV, MP4 or MOV, or check that the file plays in the Files app.

Managing storage & data

  • Transcripts and recordings: delete individual items from History.
  • Tags: rename, merge or delete them from Manage Tags.
  • Models: delete downloaded models in Settings to free up space.
  • Everything: deleting the app removes its data from the device.

Contact

For bug reports, questions or feature requests, please open an issue on GitHub. A GitHub account is required.

Open an issue on GitHub

Issues are public. Please don't include personal information, private recordings or transcripts. If it helps, you can attach logs from Settings → Diagnostics → Share Logs after checking their contents.
