How it works

From audio to transcript

Record or import

Tap to record with the microphone, or pick an audio or video file from Files or Photos. You can pause and resume while recording.

Read, edit and export

Review the text with timestamps, fix any mistakes, then share it as TXT, CSV, JSON or SRT subtitles.

Features

What you can do with it

A closer look at the main parts of the app.

Capture

Record or bring your own files

Start a recording with one tap, or import something you already have. The app converts the audio and transcribes it on the device.

  • Microphone recording with pause and resume, and a microphone switcher for external mics
  • Recording status on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island via a Live Activity (on supported iPhones)
  • Import audio and video such as M4A, WAV, MP3, MP4 and MOV from the Files app
  • Pick a video straight from your Photos library
  • Optional "Keep Screen On" for long sessions
Recognition

Whisper, running on your device

The app uses OpenAI's open Whisper models through whisper.cpp, with Metal GPU acceleration where available. You can trade speed for accuracy by picking a different model.

  • 10 Whisper models, from Tiny (about 15 MB) to Large v3 Turbo and Medium (up to about 1.5 GB)
  • Automatic language detection, or choose from 18 languages
  • Optional translation of the result into English
  • Optional Core ML encoder download that may speed up processing on supported devices
  • On iOS 26 or later, Apple's on-device speech engine is also available as an alternative
Read & edit

Timeline or reading view

Switch between a clean text view and a timeline with timestamps. Play back the original audio to check what was said, and correct the text when needed.

  • Text and timeline views, with optional timestamps
  • Audio playback with adjustable speed and position
  • Edit the transcript and its title
  • Copy a single paragraph or the whole text
  • Search inside a transcript
Organize

A searchable history

Every transcript is saved on your device so you can come back to it later.

  • Search by title, text or tag
  • Favorites and sorting
  • Tags you can add, rename, merge and delete across all transcripts
  • Filter the history by tag
Export

Text, data or subtitles

Share a transcript in the format that fits the next step — notes, a spreadsheet, a script or a video editor.

  • TXT for notes and documents
  • CSV for spreadsheets
  • JSON with segments for your own tools
  • SRT subtitles for video editing
  • Share the original recording as an audio file

Ask about a transcript iOS 26+

On devices that support Apple Intelligence, you can chat with a transcript — ask for a summary, key decisions or action items — and generate a title. Chats are saved per transcript. Long transcripts are read in sections. An optional Private Cloud Compute mode (iOS 27 or later) is off by default.

Shortcuts & Siri

Use App Shortcuts to start or stop a recording, transcribe a media file, find transcriptions, get a transcript's text, or add tags and favorites — handy for automations. Some actions need a newer version of iOS.

Privacy

Designed to keep your audio on your device

Whisper transcription runs locally. The app doesn't include ads, analytics SDKs or third-party tracking, and it doesn't send your audio or transcripts to the developer.

Stays on your device

Recordings, imported media, transcripts, tags, chats and settings are stored in the app on your device. You can delete them in the app at any time.

When the app connects

To download Whisper, VAD and Core ML model files from Hugging Face; to check the App Store for a newer version; and when iOS downloads speech models for Apple's engine.

Your choice

Private Cloud Compute for Apple Intelligence is off by default. If you turn it on, transcript text and your question are processed by Apple's Private Cloud Compute. Sharing an export sends it wherever you choose.

Requirements

Before you start

Device & system

iPhone or iPad with iOS / iPadOS 17.0 or later. Live Transcribe, Apple's speech engine and on-device Apple Intelligence need iOS 26 or later; Private Cloud Compute needs iOS 27 or later.

Storage & network

An internet connection and free storage are needed to download a model (about 15 MB to 1.5 GB depending on the model). Microphone permission is needed for recording.

Please note: AI transcripts can contain mistakes, missing words or misheard names. Check important content against the original audio, and follow local laws and get consent before recording other people. See the disclaimer for details.
FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is the app really free?

Yes. At the moment every feature is free to use. There are no ads, no in-app purchases, no subscriptions and no account sign-up.

Does it work offline?

Whisper transcription is designed to run on your device. You need an internet connection once to download a model; after that, recording and transcribing with that model can usually be done without a connection. Downloading other models, checking for updates and the optional Private Cloud Compute mode need a connection.

Is my audio sent to a server?

Whisper transcription runs locally and the app doesn't send audio or transcripts to the developer. The only exception you control is the optional Private Cloud Compute mode for Apple Intelligence chat, which sends transcript text and your question to Apple's Private Cloud Compute. It is off by default.

What files can I import and export?

You can import common audio and video formats such as M4A, WAV, MP3, MP4 and MOV from Files, and videos from Photos. Transcripts can be exported as TXT, CSV, JSON or SRT, and recordings can be shared as audio files.

Which languages are supported?

For transcription you can use automatic detection or choose English, Japanese, Chinese, Korean, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese, Russian, Arabic, Hindi, Dutch, Polish, Turkish, Vietnamese, Thai or Indonesian. Results may vary by language. The app's interface is available in 11 languages.

Can it create subtitles for a video?

Yes. Import a video, transcribe it, then export as SRT. Most video editors can import SRT files. Subtitle timing follows the recognized segments, so you may want to adjust it in your editor.

How long can a recording be?

The app doesn't set a fixed limit. In practice, very long audio depends on your device's free storage, memory and battery, and larger models take longer. If a long file fails, a smaller model or Flash Attention may help.

Is this an official OpenAI app?

No. This is an independent app that runs OpenAI's openly released Whisper models through the open-source whisper.cpp project. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by OpenAI.
