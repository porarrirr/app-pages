Smartphone Sensor Compare Support

Smartphone Sensor Compare is an iOS app that compares the effective sensor area and light-gathering power of smartphone cameras based on sensor size, focal length, and aperture.

How to use

  1. Add the devices you want to compare.
  2. Enter the focal length, aperture, and sensor information for each lens.
  3. Tap "Generate / Update Graph" to view the comparison graph and detailed values.
  4. Save presets or share comparison images as needed.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to sign in?

No, sign-in is not required.

Is my input sent to external servers?

No. Your entries and settings are stored locally on your device.

What is the Photo Library permission used for?

It is used only when you save a comparison image.
