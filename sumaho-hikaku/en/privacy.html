2. On-Device Data Storage

For your convenience, the App stores the device names, lens settings, custom sensor data, presets, and display settings you enter in your device's local storage (such as UserDefaults). This data is never sent to external servers, and the Developer cannot access it.

3. Photo Library Access

The App requests permission to add images to your Photo Library (NSPhotoLibraryAddUsageDescription) only when you choose to save a comparison image to your device. This permission is write-only for images and is never used to read your existing photos or videos.

The App does not provide, sell, or share any information about users with any third party, including advertising networks, analytics services, or partner companies.
