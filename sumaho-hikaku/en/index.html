Smartphone Sensor Compare

An iOS app that lets you compare the effective sensor area and light-gathering power of smartphone cameras based on sensor size, focal length, and aperture.

Download on the App Store Support Privacy Policy Disclaimer

Features

  • Compare multiple devices and multi-lens configurations
  • Graphs of effective sensor area and light-gathering power
  • Detailed specs such as sensor size and pixel pitch
  • Save device configuration presets
  • Share comparison images

Data handling

This app does not require sign-in, and it contains no ads, analytics, or tracking. Your entries and settings are stored locally on your device and are never sent to external servers.

Sensor specifications and calculation results are provided for reference and comparison only. Actual image quality and performance vary depending on the device, lens, and shooting conditions.
