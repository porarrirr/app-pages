Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled in Photo Cleanup (the “App”) for iPhone and iPad.

At a glance

  • All photo comparison happens on your device.
  • Photos and check results are never sent to anyone, including the developer.
  • No personal information is collected, and no account is required.
  • No ads, analytics, or tracking.

1. Information the App accesses

To find and organize exact duplicates and similar photos, the App accesses the photos and videos in your photo library to the extent you allow. You can change this access in iOS Settings at any time.

  • Image data, file size, capture date, and type of photos and videos (for example, whether an item is a screenshot)
  • Whether a photo is a favorite (so that favorites are protected from deletion)

2. Information stored on your device

So that you can resume organizing where you left off, the App stores the following information in its own private area on your device. This information is never sent anywhere and is excluded from iCloud Backup. Deleting the App also deletes this information.

  • Check results (fingerprint data used to compare photos, file sizes, and similar data)
  • Organizing progress (your Keep, Delete, Decide later, and Protect choices, where you left off, and similar data)
  • App settings (such as whether “Include iCloud Photos” is on)

3. Data transmission and network use

The App has no connection to any server run by the developer. It never sends photos, check results, or usage information anywhere.

When “Include iCloud Photos” is on, iOS may download photos from Apple's iCloud to your device so that photos not stored on the device can be shown and compared. This transfer is performed by Apple's services and is subject to Apple's privacy policy.

The App also checks your device's network connection status to show whether you are offline. This information is used only on your device.

4. Deleting photos

The App deletes photos only when you start the deletion from the delete list and approve it on the iOS confirmation screen. Deleted photos can be recovered from Recently Deleted in the Photos app for a while. If you use iCloud Photos, they are also removed from your other devices signed in to the same Apple Account.

5. Sharing, advertising, and tracking

Because the App collects no information, it shares none with third parties. It contains no external services or SDKs for advertising, analytics, or crash reporting, and it does not engage in tracking as defined by App Tracking Transparency.

6. Children's privacy

The App does not collect personal information from anyone, regardless of age.

7. Your choices

You can change or revoke photo access at any time in the Settings app. Deleting the App removes all check results and organizing progress stored on your device.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as needed. If there are significant changes, we will announce them on this page and update the effective date.

9. Contact

For questions about this policy, please contact us using the method described on the support page.
