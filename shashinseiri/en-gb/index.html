▸ 01A▸ 01A — 01BKeep/Delete

Find → Compare → Confirm & delete

▸ 01 — 03

Similar photos start marked “Keep.” Tap only the ones you want to delete, then review everything before deleting.

  1. 01

    Find

    Tap “Start checking” and the app automatically finds exact duplicates and similar shots like bursts and retakes on your device. Close the app anytime and pick up where you left off.

  2. 02

    Compare

    Similar photos start marked “Keep.” Compare shots side by side and tap the photos you want to delete to switch between “Keep” and “Delete.” You can also zoom in to check details.

  3. 03

    Confirm and delete

    Photos marked “Delete” gather in the delete list — nothing is deleted yet. Review the count and total size, confirm in the app, and tap “Delete” on your iPhone's confirmation screen.

Duplicates, bursts, screenshots, videos, and by month

▸ 01 — 05

Beyond duplicates and bursts, review screenshots, large videos, and your photo library month by month.

  1. 01

    Exact duplicates

    Finds and groups photos whose files are completely identical. Keeps one suggested copy plus any copy in your albums, and lets you organize remaining groups at once.

  2. 02

    Bursts and similar photos

    Groups shots that look nearly the same, such as bursts and retakes. Every photo starts marked “Keep,” so you simply tap the ones you want to delete.

  3. 03

    Screenshots

    See all your screenshots in one list. Tap “Select” at the top right to choose several at once and send them to the delete list.

  4. 04

    Large videos

    Lists videos from largest to smallest. Deleting large videos is the quickest way to free up storage space.

  5. 05

    Browse by month

    Review photos one by one, newest first, month by month. Track your progress for each month and continue straight to the next.

Built to prevent accidents

▸ 01 — 04
  • ▸ 01

    Nothing is deleted right away

    Adding photos to the delete list doesn't delete them. When you delete, you confirm in the app and once more on your iPhone's confirmation screen.

  • ▸ 02

    Favorites and “Protect”

    Your favorite photos in Photos, and any photo you mark with “Protect” in the large view, can't be deleted.

  • ▸ 03

    Recover later

    Deleted photos can be recovered from Recently Deleted in the Photos app for a while.

  • ▸ 04

    Undo the last action

    Picked the wrong one? Tap × to remove a photo from the delete list, or tap “Undo” to revert your last saved choice or protection change.

Your photos stay on your device

All photo comparison happens on your iPhone or iPad. Photos and check results are never sent anywhere. No account is needed, and there are no ads or tracking.

Frequently asked questions

▸ 01 — 06

Answers on tapping to choose, deletion behavior, favorites protection, recovery, iCloud Photos, and pricing.

01When I tap a photo, do I tap to keep it or tap to delete it?+

Each tap switches a photo between “Keep” and “Delete.” In “Similar photos,” every shot starts marked “Keep,” so you tap the photos you want to delete. In “Exact duplicates,” one suggested copy plus any copy in your albums starts marked “Keep” and the rest start marked “Delete,” and you can tap any photo to switch it.

02Are photos deleted as soon as I choose “Delete”?+

No. Photos you choose to delete only gather in the delete list; they haven't been deleted yet. They're deleted only after you review the count and total size in the delete list, confirm in the app, and tap “Delete” on your iPhone's confirmation screen. Storage space is freed after the photos are also removed from Recently Deleted in the Photos app.

03Are favorite photos and protected photos safe from deletion?+

Yes. Photos you've marked as favorites in the Photos app are protected automatically and can't be deleted. You can also open any photo in the large view and choose “Protect.” If a photo already on the delete list is edited or added to Favorites in Photos, it is automatically removed from the delete list.

04Can I undo a choice or get back a photo I deleted by mistake?+

Before deleting, tap × in the delete list to remove any photo, or tap “Undo” to revert your last saved choice or protection change. After deleting, photos can be recovered from Recently Deleted in the Photos app for a while before they are permanently deleted.

05I use iCloud Photos. How do checking and deleting work?+

If you use iCloud Photos, deleted photos are also removed from your other devices signed in to the same Apple Account, such as your iPad or Mac (and from every participant's library in an iCloud Shared Photo Library). Turn on “Include iCloud Photos” in Settings to check photos stored in iCloud: during a check, downloads run only on Wi‑Fi (not in Low Data Mode) and pause automatically while free device storage is below 2 GB, checking only on-device photos until space is freed.

06How much does Photo Cleanup cost?+

Photo Cleanup is free. There are no in-app purchases, subscriptions, ads, tracking, or account requirements.
