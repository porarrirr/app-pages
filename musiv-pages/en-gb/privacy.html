Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 22, 2026

Musiv (the “App”) is a utility that converts shared music-service links into search and playback links for other services. The App does not stream, download, or redistribute music. This policy explains the information handled through the App; each third-party music service’s own privacy policy applies to that service.

Data we collect and store

  • Conversion history — track titles, artists, original URLs, etc., stored on your device (up to 50 entries). They are not sent to or stored on the developer’s servers.
  • Settings — your preferred subscription and the clipboard auto-convert on/off setting, stored on your device.
  • Share sheet integration — when you share, the music URL is passed temporarily to the main app via an App Group and deleted afterwards.

Depending on device loss, backups, or integration with other apps, on-device data could be read by third parties, depending on the OS and your usage environment. The developer does not guarantee the security of your device.

Clipboard

The App reads the clipboard to check whether the copied text is a music link. Content other than music links may also be read temporarily. If you turn off “Automatic Redirect” in the app’s settings, links are not converted automatically.

Network

To convert links, the App connects to third-party operated services such as the music pages you specify, Apple’s iTunes Search API, and YouTube’s oEmbed, and retrieves publicly available metadata (track titles, artist names, etc.). The developer does not control or manage those third parties’ data handling and does not guarantee the accuracy of the retrieved information. The developer never sells or provides such information to third parties for advertising-profile purposes.

Tracking and third-party sharing

The App does not perform advertising tracking or behavioral advertising by the developer. Data collected by the App is never sold to third parties. (Tracking on each music service follows that service’s own policy.)

Children

The App is not directed at children under 13. If a minor uses the App without parental consent, it is used at the parent’s responsibility.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be changed without notice. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the changes.

Disclaimer

For matters not covered by this policy, the Terms of Service & Disclaimer applies.

Contact
