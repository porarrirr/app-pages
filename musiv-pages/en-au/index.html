Open music links with the subscription you already have

Musiv is a utility that turns track links copied from Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube Music, Amazon Music, and more into search and link guidance for the service you choose.

This app does not stream, download, or redistribute music. It does not guarantee the accuracy of conversions or playback on each service. Use at your own discretion.

What it can do

  • Automatically detects and converts music links on your clipboard (can be turned off in Settings)
  • Convert from the share sheet, Safari extension, or Shortcuts
  • Conversion history (stored on your device, up to 50 entries; never sent to the developer's servers)
