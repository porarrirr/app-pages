Dual mode

Compare two AI models side by side on iPhone and iPad

In Dual mode, YamabikoChat sends the same prompt to two models at once and shows both answers next to each other — so you can see where they agree, where they differ, and which one fits your task.

Last updated: 2026-11-17

How Dual mode works

  1. Connect your providers. Add at least one AI service in Settings. You can compare two models from the same provider or from two different providers.
  2. Start a chat in Dual mode. Choose Dual when you start a new conversation.
  3. Pick model A and model B. For example, a GPT model against a Gemini model, or Claude against DeepSeek through OpenRouter.
  4. Send your prompt. Both answers stream in at the same time, side by side. Open either answer on its own, and retry if a request fails.

What comparing answers is good for

  • Checking facts. When two models disagree, you know exactly which claim to verify in a primary source.
  • Writing and translation. See two drafts, tones or translations of the same text and keep the better one.
  • Code and math. Compare two solutions or explanations of the same problem.
  • Choosing a model. Find out which model handles your kind of question best before you rely on it — or pay for heavy use.

Let two models talk to each other

Auto conversation goes one step further: choose two models, a topic and a number of turns, and the models take turns responding to each other. Each turn streams live in its own speaker view. Use it to debate the pros and cons of a decision, brainstorm from two angles, or rehearse an interview.

Delegate parts of a task

When subagents are turned on, the model can delegate parts of a request to child agents — each can use a different model — and you can follow their progress in a compact summary in the chat.

Which models can I compare?

Any model available from the providers you have connected — including Google Gemini, OpenRouter's model directory, your ChatGPT plan, xAI, OpenAI, Anthropic, DeepSeek, Mistral and Groq. See supported providers.

Each side of a Dual chat is a separate request, billed by its provider. Models differ in capabilities such as image input and context length, so the same attachment may not work on both sides.
