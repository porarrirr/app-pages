Bring your own AI · iPhone & iPad

AI chat on your own API key. Compare the answers.

YamabikoChat is a free AI chat app for iPhone and iPad. Connect the AI services you already use — with an API key or a supported account — then chat with one model, compare two models side by side, or let two models talk to each other.

At a glance

What YamabikoChat is

Type
Native AI chat client for iPhone and iPad that uses your own API keys or accounts (BYOK)
Price
Free. No ads, no sign-up, no subscriptions or token purchases in the app. Provider usage is billed by the provider.
AI services
Google Gemini, OpenRouter, ChatGPT plan, xAI, Apple Intelligence, OpenAI, Anthropic (Claude), DeepSeek, Mistral, Groq, Sarashina, PLaMo, custom endpoints and more — see all providers
Modes
Chat, Dual (two models side by side) and Auto conversation (two models talk to each other), plus subagents that take on parts of a task
Privacy
Conversations stay on your device, keys are kept in the iOS Keychain, and prompts go only to the provider you choose
Requirements
iOS 17 or later (iPhone), iPadOS 17 or later (iPad)
Developer
Independent developer porarrirr · open source under the MIT Licence

Modes

Three conversation modes, plus subagents

Pick the mode that fits the question, not the other way around.

01 / Chat

Talk to one model

Streaming answers with Markdown, code highlighting, math and image attachments. Edit, regenerate and keep response variants.

02 / Dual

Compare two answers

Send the same prompt to two models and read both answers side by side. How Dual works

03 / Auto

Let two models talk

Choose two models, a topic and a number of turns, then watch them discuss it with each other.

+ / Subagents

Delegate the work

Let the model hand parts of a request to subagents — each can use its own model — and follow their progress in the chat.

Features

Built for everyday work

Writing, summaries, translation, code and math — with the tools a real assistant needs.

  • Attach images, PDFs and text files (up to 10 MB each)
  • Web search, local Python and interactive charts (GenUI) as tools
  • Voice dictation and a share extension for Safari and other apps
  • Shortcuts actions, Spotlight search and secret conversations
  • Projects, history search, model presets and token usage statistics
  • iPad split view and dark mode

Who it's for

Who it's for

  • You already pay for an API key or a ChatGPT plan and want a native iPhone and iPad app for it.
  • You want to check one model's answer against another before you trust it.
  • You'd rather pay per use than stack several AI subscriptions.
  • You use OpenRouter or several providers and want them in one place.
  • You want your chat history kept on your own device.
