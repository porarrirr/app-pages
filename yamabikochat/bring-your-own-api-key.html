Bring your own key

Use your own API key for AI chat on iPhone and iPad

YamabikoChat is a bring-your-own-key (BYOK) client. You add an API key — or sign in to a supported account — and the app sends your prompts straight to that provider. There is no YamabikoChat subscription or token reseller in between.

Last updated: 2026-11-17

What “bring your own key” means

AI providers such as OpenAI, Google, Anthropic and OpenRouter sell access to their models through API keys, usually billed per use. A BYOK app is a client for those keys: you create a key in your provider account, add it to the app, and the provider bills you directly for what you use.

Why use your own key

  • Pay for what you use. Light users often spend less than a monthly AI subscription; heavy users see exactly where the cost comes from.
  • Choose the model. Use the models your provider offers, not a fixed list chosen by an app.
  • One app, many providers. Keep Gemini, OpenRouter, OpenAI, Anthropic and others side by side, and compare them in Dual mode.
  • See your usage. Token usage statistics show how much you use.

How to set it up

  1. Create an API key in your provider's console — for example Google AI Studio for Gemini, the OpenAI platform, the Anthropic console, or OpenRouter.
  2. Open Settings in YamabikoChat and choose the provider in the connection settings.
  3. Paste the key. It is saved in the iOS Keychain on your device.
  4. Pick a model and start chatting. Models the app cannot run safely are shown as unavailable, with the reason, instead of being guessed.

No API key? Sign in instead

  • ChatGPT plan — sign in with your ChatGPT account to use the models included in your plan.
  • xAI — sign in with your xAI account.
  • Apple Intelligence — built in on supported devices, on-device or through Private Cloud Compute, with no key at all.

Custom endpoints

If your service is not in the list, add a custom endpoint with its base URL and key, and choose one of the supported API formats: OpenAI Chat Completions, OpenAI Responses, Anthropic Messages, Gemini (Google Generative AI) or Mistral.

What it costs

The app is free, with no in-app purchases. Your provider charges for usage under its own pricing, limits and terms. Dual mode and Auto conversation send requests to two models, so they create usage on both.

Security and privacy

  • API keys and access tokens are stored in the iOS Keychain, and the app is designed not to write them to its diagnostic logs.
  • Prompts, conversation context and the attachments you choose are sent directly to the provider you selected.
  • Conversations are stored on your device. The app does not use cross-app tracking. See the privacy policy.
