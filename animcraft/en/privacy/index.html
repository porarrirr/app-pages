Privacy Policy

AnimCraft is engineered around one strict rule: your media never leaves your device. We do not collect personal data, run analytics, serve ads, or operate a cloud backend.

01

100% On-Device Processing

Decoding, Metal rendering, AI background removal, upscaling, interpolation, tracking, and speech captions all run locally.

02

Zero Trackers or Ad SDKs

No analytics SDKs, no advertising identifiers, no crash uploaders, no telemetry, and no account registration.

03

No Developer Backend

We operate no servers that receive, store, or process your photos, videos, GIFs, or projects.

04

Built-In Metadata Stripping

Toggle Preserve Metadata off in Export Studio or Batch Studio to strip EXIF and location tags before sharing.

01

Core Policy & Data We Do Not Collect

AnimCraft (“the App”) is designed to protect your privacy by default. The App does not collect, transmit, sell, or share your name, email address, location, device identifiers (such as IDFA), usage analytics, crash diagnostics, or any photos, videos, GIFs, audio, or projects you import or edit.

The App contains zero third-party advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, or behavioral tracking libraries.

02

Local On-Device Storage & App Group Container

To support non-destructive editing, crash recovery, custom libraries, and Apple system extensions, the App stores the following data strictly inside your device’s private sandbox and local App Group container (group.com.animcraft.studio):

  • ▪ Active projects, autosave crash-recovery journals, and trashed projects awaiting restore or deletion
  • ▪ Temporary frame and thumbnail caches (clearable anytime from Settings)
  • ▪ User-imported custom fonts (.ttf / .otf), 3D LUTs (.cube), custom stickers, and saved export presets
  • ▪ Media handed off locally from the AnimCraft Share Extension and animated stickers saved for the AnimCraft Stickers iMessage Extension

None of this local data is sent to the developer. You may delete individual projects, empty Trash, or clear caches inside the App at any time. Uninstalling the App removes its local sandbox data in accordance with iOS behavior.

If you enable iCloud Backup or encrypted Mac/PC backups on your device, iOS may include local app data according to Apple’s backup policies.

03

Device Permissions & System Frameworks

AnimCraft requests system permissions only when you explicitly invoke the corresponding feature:

  • ▪ Photo Library (Read / Add): Used solely to import photos, videos, Live Photos, and GIFs you select, and to save exported files back to Photos.
  • ▪ Camera: Used only when you launch the in-app Camera Capture workflow.
  • ▪ Speech Recognition (SFSpeechRecognizer): Used only when you run Auto Captions. AnimCraft strictly enforces requiresOnDeviceRecognition = true so audio is transcribed entirely on-device without being sent to any server.
  • ▪ Clipboard: Read or written only when you explicitly tap Clipboard Import or Copy.
  • ▪ Screen Recording (ReplayKit) & Apple Image Playground: Triggered exclusively on user action through standard Apple system frameworks.
04

Optional “Import from URL” Network Request

AnimCraft works completely offline by default. The sole network capability in the App is the optional “Import from URL” feature, which connects directly from your device (via URLSession) to the exact http:// or https:// URL you manually enter.

Traffic never passes through any server operated by the developer. Standard technical logs (such as IP address) received by the third-party host you choose are governed by that host’s privacy policy.

05

Exporting, Metadata Control & Sharing

Exporting, saving to Files, AirDrop, and sharing to other apps occur only upon your explicit tap. Both Export Studio and Batch Studio include a Preserve Metadata toggle so you can strip EXIF/location metadata before output.

Once you share a file to another app or service via the iOS Share Sheet, that destination service’s privacy policy governs how it handles the file.

06

Support & Privacy Emails

If you email support@rarari.org or privacy@rarari.org, we receive your sender address, message body, and any attachments you choose to include. We use this information solely to respond to your inquiry, investigate reported bugs, and maintain necessary support records.

We never sell or share support correspondence for marketing or advertising. You may request review or deletion of past email correspondence by writing to privacy@rarari.org.

07

This Website & GitHub Pages Hosting

This website (marketing, support, and privacy pages) contains zero analytics scripts, zero ad tags, and zero tracking cookies. Browser localStorage is used solely if you toggle Light/Dark appearance.

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical request metadata such as IP addresses for content delivery and infrastructure security in accordance with the GitHub General Privacy Statement. Apple platform services (such as the App Store and iCloud Backup) are governed by the Apple Privacy Policy.

08

Children’s Privacy & Policy Changes

Because AnimCraft does not collect personal information from any user, no personal data is collected from children.

If we update this Privacy Policy to reflect app updates or legal changes, we will publish the revised policy on this page and update the Last Updated date below.
