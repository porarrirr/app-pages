ON-DEVICE AI & METAL ENGINE — ZERO-CLOUD LOCAL INFERENCE

VISION · VIDEOTOOLBOX ML · METAL

Every AI model executes locally on your iPhone or iPad via Apple Vision, VideoToolbox ML, and custom Metal compute shaders. Your photos and videos are never sent to external AI servers.

SEGMENTATION LOCAL

AI Background Removal + Temporal Stabilization + Manual Brush

Runs Vision Foreground Instance Mask (people, animals, objects) and Person Segmentation with edge feathering, morphological shrink/expand, custom Metal temporal mask stabilization to stop flicker, and manual Keep/Erase brushes.

SUPER RESOLUTION LOCAL

Alpha-Aware 2× / 4× AI Super Resolution

Uses Apple VideoToolbox ML (VTLowLatencySuperResolutionScaler) on physical iOS 26+ Apple Silicon devices, and progressive Lanczos + Metal bilateral denoise + Contrast-Adaptive Sharpening on older devices. Edge-dilated RGB+Alpha split/recombine keeps transparent GIFs and stickers free of dark halos.

OPTICAL FLOW LOCAL

Scene-Cut Protected 2× / 4× Frame Interpolation

Bidirectional Vision dense optical flow (A→B & B→A) + custom Metal occlusion-aware warping (and VideoToolbox ML on iOS 26+ hardware) for smooth slow-motion or higher FPS, guarded by automatic histogram + structural scene-cut detection.

TRACKING LOCAL

Forward & Backward Object & Face Tracking

Tracks general objects (VNTrackObjectRequest) or faces (VNDetectFaceRectanglesRequest with IoU + centroid matching) to generate editable layer keyframes for text, stickers, or privacy redaction masks.

MOTION LAB LOCAL

Seamless Loop Finder, Stabilization & Cinemagraphs

Automatic best-loop range discovery, Apple Vision Translational Video Stabilization, Subject-Centered Auto-Reframe, Cinemagraph selective freeze brush masks, and Ken Burns Pan & Zoom.

APPLE INTELLIGENCE LOCAL

Native Apple Image Playground Integration

On Apple Intelligence-capable devices, generate AI Stickers, Genmoji, AI Backgrounds, and AI Restyle Morphs directly inside the editor without leaving AnimCraft.

PRECISION GIF COMPRESSION — REAL-ENCODE TARGET SIZE OPTIMIZER & BENCHMARKS

4 QUANTIZERS × 5 DITHERS

Need a GIF strictly under Discord’s 512 KB cap, Slack’s 128 KB emoji limit, or GitHub’s 5 MB demo ceiling? Instead of guessing, AnimCraft’s TargetSizeOptimizer runs real candidate GIF encodes across a binary/Pareto quality ladder to guarantee actualBytes <= targetBytes.

01

4 Color Quantization Algorithms

Xiaolin Wu 3D variance-minimizing quantizer, Median Cut, Octree (crisp UI/text/pixel art), and K-Means++ refiner accelerated by a 6-6-6 centered perceptual color LUT.

02

5 Dithering Engines + Temporal Dither Lock

None, serpentine Floyd-Steinberg, Atkinson, Sierra Lite (with adaptive anti-noise deadzone), subtle 4×4 Ordered Bayer, plus Temporal Dither Lock to eliminate shimmering static backgrounds.

03

Deep GIF89a Bitstream Optimization

Gifsicle-style Lossy LZW (0–160), sub-rectangle delta compression, transparent unchanged-pixel masking, automatic Disposal Method switching, duplicate frame merging with duration preservation, and Global/Local/Adaptive Hybrid palettes.

FAQ — FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

AI SEARCH & USER Q&A
Q01 Is AnimCraft genuinely free with no watermarks, ads, or hidden subscriptions? ▸

Yes. AnimCraft is 100% free with zero ads, zero in-app purchases, zero subscriptions, zero account sign-in, zero watermarks, and no artificial caps on frame count, resolution, or clip length.

Q02 Does AnimCraft upload my videos or photos to a server for GIF encoding or AI background removal? ▸

Never. All media decoding, Metal rendering, Apple Vision and VideoToolbox ML AI inference, on-device speech recognition (requiresOnDeviceRecognition = true), encoding, and backups execute strictly on your iPhone or iPad.

Q03 How does AnimCraft compress a GIF under Discord (512 KB) or Slack (128 KB) limits? ▸

Select a built-in preset (Discord Sticker ≤ 512 KB, Slack Emoji ≤ 128 KB) or enter any custom KB target. TargetSizeOptimizer executes real candidate GIF encodes combining Xiaolin Wu/Octree quantization, sub-rectangle delta compression, Temporal Dither Lock, and Lossy LZW until actualBytes <= targetBytes.

Q04 Can I make LINE animated stickers (APNG) or WhatsApp/Telegram animated stickers (WebP) on iPhone? ▸

Yes. AnimCraft includes native APNG and Google libwebp Animated WebP encoders, dedicated presets for LINE (320×270 APNG ≤ 300 KB), WhatsApp (512×512 WebP ≤ 500 KB), Telegram (512px ≤ 256 KB), Discord, and Slack, plus a built-in Sticker Spec Validator.

Q05 What iOS / iPadOS versions and hardware are supported? ▸

AnimCraft supports iPhone and iPad running iOS 18.0 / iPadOS 18.0 or later. VideoToolbox ML hardware super resolution and frame interpolation automatically activate on physical iOS 26+ Apple Silicon devices (A17 Pro / M-series+), while earlier devices use AnimCraft’s native Metal + Apple Vision engines. Apple Image Playground requires an Apple Intelligence-compatible device.

Q06 Can AnimCraft export transparent video, Live Photos, or game sprite sheets? ▸

Yes. AnimCraft natively exports 15 formats including HEVC with Alpha (.mov), Apple Live Photos (paired JPEG + MOV with Lock Screen preview), Sprite Sheet PNG + JSON Atlas with customizable columns/padding, PNG/JPEG Sequence ZIPs, and Still AVIF/WebP/PNG/JPEG.

Q07 How can I back up or transfer my projects without cloud storage? ▸

You can share individual .animcraft project packages anytime, or open Settings to create a complete local ZIP backup (projects, custom fonts, 3D LUTs, and stickers) and restore it on another device without any cloud account.
