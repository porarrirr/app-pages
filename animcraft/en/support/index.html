QUICK GUIDES — GETTING STARTED

STEP-BY-STEP
01

Convert Video, Photos, or Live Photos into GIF

  1. 1 Tap 01 Video to GIF, 02 Photos to GIF, or 04 Live Photo to GIF on the Home screen.
  2. 2 Choose media from Photos or Files (you can also Drag & Drop, paste from Clipboard, or send via the iOS Share Sheet).
  3. 3 Apply trims, speed curves, text, filters, or AI background removal in the editor, then tap Export to choose any of the 15 output formats.
02

Compress a GIF Under an Exact File Size Limit (128 KB / 512 KB / 5 MB)

  1. 1 Open Export Studio, choose GIF, and enable Target File Size Auto-Optimizer.
  2. 2 Pick a quick size chip (128 KB, 256 KB, 512 KB, 1 MB, 5 MB, 10 MB) or enter a custom KB limit, then select a priority policy (Balanced, Resolution, Smoothness, Color Fidelity, or Smallest Size).
  3. 3 After encoding, inspect the live Before/After split comparison, exact byte size, and PSNR/SSIM before saving to Photos, Files, or Clipboard.
03

Create LINE, WhatsApp, Telegram, Discord, or iMessage Stickers

  1. 1 Use AI Background Removal or Chroma Key to make the background transparent, and optionally add a Die-Cut Sticker Outline.
  2. 2 Choose the matching built-in preset in Export Studio (LINE Animated APNG, WhatsApp 512px WebP, Telegram, Discord, or Slack) and verify compliance with the built-in Sticker Spec Validator.
  3. 3 For iMessage, save exported animated stickers to the local sticker vault and send them via AnimCraft Stickers inside Apple Messages.
04

Local Full Backup, Restore & Storage Cleanup

  1. 1 Open Settings (gear icon on Home) to view exact storage usage across Projects, Cache, Custom Fonts, Stickers, and 3D LUTs.
  2. 2 Tap Clear Cache to reclaim thumbnail cache space safely without deleting any project.
  3. 3 Use Create Full Backup to export a complete local ZIP archive and Restore Backup on any iPhone or iPad without cloud accounts.

TROUBLESHOOTING — COMMON QUESTIONS

DIAGNOSTICS
T01 Importing a video or photo fails or takes a long time ▸

Media stored only in iCloud Photos must finish downloading to the device first. Check your connection and free device storage, then try again. Corrupted or DRM-protected videos cannot be imported.

T02 Target file size optimizer reports the target KB is unreachable ▸

Long, high-resolution, or high-motion clips can hit the physical lower bound of GIF encoding. Try trimming the duration, lowering output width, setting FPS to 10–15 fps, or switching priority policy to Smallest Size.

T03 On-Device Auto Captions is unavailable ▸

For 100% privacy, AnimCraft enforces requiresOnDeviceRecognition = true so audio is never uploaded. Ensure Speech Recognition permission is granted and that Apple’s on-device dictation model for your language is installed in iOS Settings > General > Keyboard > Dictation.

T04 AI Background Removal edges look rough or miss a small detail ▸

Adjust edge feathering, shrink/expand, and enable Metal Temporal Mask Stabilization inside the AI tool. For fine details, paint directly on the canvas with the manual Keep and Erase mask brushes.

T05 AnimCraft Stickers does not appear in iMessage ▸

Launch AnimCraft once after installation, then open Messages > + > More to enable AnimCraft Stickers. On some iOS versions, restarting the device refreshes newly installed Messages extensions.
