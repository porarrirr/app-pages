For iPhone and iPad with iOS 18 or later

What it does

Set by size

Just enter a limit, like '25 MB or less'. The app checks the actual size of the exported file and, if it's over, adjusts the settings and exports again automatically.

Common limits in one tap

Pick the limits of popular services such as Discord, Gmail, Slack, WhatsApp and Mail Drop from presets.

Set by quality

Prefer not to pick a size? Choose 'Small', 'Balanced' or 'High quality' instead.

Compare before saving

Compare the compressed video with the original, then save it to Photos, save it to Files, or share it.

Compress in batches

Add several videos at once and compress them one after another with the same settings. You can also add videos from the Share menu.

Fine-tune format and quality

Supports H.264, HEVC, AV1, ProRes 422 Proxy and Motion JPEG. Choose resolution, frame rate and audio too. HEVC can keep HDR.

Your videos never leave your device

All compression happens on your iPhone or iPad. Videos are never uploaded to a server, and there's no account sign-up, no ads and no analytics. Location data is removed from the output by default.
