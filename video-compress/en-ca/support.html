Support

Answers to common questions about using Video Compress and fixing problems. If you can't find what you need, please contact us by email.

Contact

Send questions, bug reports and feature requests to the email address below.

support@rarari.org

When reporting a problem, the following details help us investigate:

  • App version
  • Device model and iOS / iPadOS version
  • The settings you chose (target size or quality, format, resolution, frame rate)
  • Length and size of the original video, and the device or app it was recorded with
  • Any error message that appeared

Please don't send video files unless we ask for them.

Frequently asked questions

The video doesn't fit within the size I set.

Video Compress checks the actual size of the exported file and, if it's over the limit, adjusts the settings and exports again, up to four times in total. If it still doesn't fit, the target size may be too small for the length of the video. Using the minimum estimate shown in the error as a guide, try a larger target size, set resolution and frame rate to "Auto (recommended)", or remove the audio.

Where are compressed videos saved?

On the result screen, choose "Save to Photos", "Save to Files", or share. Until you save it, the compressed video exists only inside the app.

I can't save to Photos.

The app may not be allowed to add to your photo library. In Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos, allow Video Compress "Add Photos Only" or "Full Access".

Is the original video deleted?

No. Video Compress compresses a copy of the original, so the original video in your photo library or the Files app is never changed or deleted.

A video won't load.

Copy-protected (DRM) videos, damaged files and formats your device can't decode can't be loaded. For videos stored in iCloud Photos, wait until they've finished downloading to your device.

Compression stopped partway through.

If you switch to another app or lock the screen while compressing, iOS may interrupt the process. Interrupted videos are shown when you open the app; keep the app open and run them again.

Which format should I choose?

If you're not sure, choose H.264. It plays in almost every app and device. HEVC gives better quality at the same size, but may not play on older devices or some services. AV1 plays only in apps and devices that support it. ProRes 422 Proxy and Motion JPEG are editing formats that can be larger than the original, and are available only when setting by quality.

What happens to HDR videos?

Exporting as H.264 converts them to SDR. To keep HDR, choose HEVC.

Are location and recording date kept?

The recording date is kept. Location is removed by default and kept only when you turn on "Keep location" in Advanced settings. No other metadata is written.

The app is using too much storage.

Tap "Manage" at the top of the screen to delete compressed videos and temporary files. Compressed videos you haven't saved will be lost, but your original videos are not deleted.

Can I add videos from other apps?

Yes. Select a video in Photos or Files, tap Share, and choose "Add to Video Compress". The video will be there the next time you open Video Compress.

Requirements

iPhone with iOS 18 or later, or iPad with iPadOS 18 or later. Available formats vary by device.
