Support & FAQ

This guide explains how to open ABC, MusicXML, and LilyPond scores in ABC Studio, and how to play, edit, and export them.

01 User guide 02 Frequently asked questions (FAQ) 03 Contact

Let's start by playing a piece

Once you launch the app, you can experience score display, playback, and editing in five quick steps.

  1. 1
    Open a score

    From the "+" at the top of the screen, choose "Open from Files", "Load from Clipboard", or "Open Sample". When you pick a file, the app detects its score format.

  2. 2
    Listen & control playback

    Tap the play button in the playback bar at the bottom to start playback. In playback settings you can set speed from 50–200% and enable loop playback. Move the position from the playback bar, or tap notes on ABC scores to jump.

  3. 3
    Adjust part volumes & instruments

    In the "Mixer" tab, set per-part volume sliders and instruments (78 types). Use "Solo" to focus on one part and "Mute" for parts you do not need. "Instrument placement" and "Adjust earphone panning" also let you position the sound.

  4. 4
    Edit the score source

    Edit the score source in the "Editor" tab and press "Apply to Score". Check diagnostics with "Validate Notation", and drafts you are typing are auto-saved.

  5. 5
    Export to files & share

    From "Export & Share" you can choose score sources, MusicXML, compressed MusicXML (MXL), MIDI, and WAV. PDF combines all pages into one; PNG makes one image per page. PDF and PNG can be previewed before sharing.

Supported formats: ABC (.abc, .txt), MusicXML (.musicxml, .xml, .mxl), LilyPond (.ly, a project folder, or a ZIP exported by ABC Studio). The input limit is 8 MiB.

FAQ

Which devices and OS versions are supported?

Runs on iPhone and iPad with iOS 17 or iPadOS 17 and later.

Score display, editing, playback, saving, and export are processed on your device. The LilyPond compiler is also bundled with the app.

What file formats can be loaded, and what are ABC notation's limits?

ABC reads UTF-8 or UTF-16 text with BOM. When one file contains multiple pieces (X:), you can switch between them from the piece selector on the score screen.

Supports multiple voices (V:), ties, tuplets, ornaments (trills, mordents, etc.), fermatas, repeat signs, and numbered endings (|1 |2). Also supports P: for specifying part playback order and & for overlapping voices within a measure.

What should I know when opening MusicXML or LilyPond scores?

MusicXML also opens compressed MXL. Unusual notation, performance directions, and fine paper layout may not display or play back exactly as in the original software, and conversion to MusicXML does not carry over every notation or layout detail.

For relative-path \include directives or images, select a folder containing main.ly or score.ly together with its related files.

How many instruments can I choose? Can choir sing lyrics?

Choose from 78 instrument assignments including piano, organ, strings such as violin and cello, winds such as flute and trumpet, guitar, and drum kit. Some instruments share the same sound based on the General MIDI (GM) specification.

Choir is performed instrumentally with the "Choir Aahs" sound; there is no feature to synthesize sung lyrics.

What should I do when there is no sound?

First check your device's media volume and output destination such as Bluetooth earphones.

In the "Mixer" tab, check "Master volume", each part's mute, and other parts' solo settings. After interruptions by calls or Siri, press the play button again to resume.

What if the score doesn't display correctly or shows errors?

Check syntax diagnostics in the "Editor" tab. In ABC, when there are errors in headers (piece number X:, title T:, time signature M:, unit note length L:, key K:) or note entries, the line number and details of the problem are shown.

When contacting us about a bug, attaching a minimal score source that reproduces the problem helps us investigate smoothly.

Can I rename or delete saved pieces?

In the "Library" tab, long-press a piece or use the "…" menu on each row to rename or delete it. A confirmation alert appears before deletion.

Can I pause auto-follow (auto-scroll) on the score?

Toggle "Follow playback position" in the "View" menu on the score screen.

On iOS/iPadOS 18 and later, manually scrolling an ABC score temporarily pauses following. Tap "Return to playback position" to resume.

Do I need an account or an internet connection?

No, none at all. ABC Studio is designed to run fully locally and never asks you to create an account, log in, or authenticate with a cloud service.

Note that sending exported files via email or cloud storage, or opening websites and inquiry emails, involves communication with those external services.
