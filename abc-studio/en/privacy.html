YOUR MUSIC STAYS YOURS

Privacy Policy

ABC Studio respects your privacy and is designed to avoid unnecessary data collection.

Last updated: November 25, 2026

Privacy principles

Fully local processing Score parsing, rendering, and audio synthesis all happen on your device and are never sent to external servers.
Zero tracking & ads No ads, behavioral tracking SDKs, or analytics libraries are built into the app.
You choose the destination Exported scores and audio are handed to the storage location or app you choose when you pick a share destination.

porari (the "Operator") explains how information is handled in ABC Studio (the "App") and the ABC Studio information site (the "Site"), as follows.

1. Data handled by this app

This app runs on iOS 17 or iPadOS 17 and later. Parsing, display, playback, and export of ABC, MusicXML, and LilyPond scores are processed on the device. LilyPond processing uses the compiler bundled with the app. The app has no account registration, ads, analytics SDKs, tracking features, or automatic crash reporting to the developer. Nothing such as scores or usage is ever sent from the app to the operator.

2. Information stored on your device

To provide features and restore state on the next launch, this app stores the following information in its on-device app storage area. None of it is sent from the app to the operator.

  • The source, filename, edits, and imported related files of the last opened score
  • Score zoom level, auto-follow of the playback position, and earphone panning settings
  • Scores and related files saved in the library, plus management info such as display names, creation dates, and piece counts

Songs in your library can be deleted individually in the app. Text being edited can be cleared with "Delete All Input" in the editor. Removing the app from your device deletes its local storage; "Offload App" keeps documents and data.

Exporting score sources, MusicXML, MXL, PDF, PNG, MIDI, WAV, etc. may create temporary files on your device. Files saved to a share destination are managed by that destination.

If you have backups to iCloud or a Mac/PC enabled, this app's local data may be included in backups. Backup encryption and management follow your device settings and Apple's service specifications. See Apple's "iCloud Backup coverage" for details.

3. Files and clipboard

The app reads score files you open and related files included in the selected project. LilyPond reference files are read only within the access the OS permits. Clipboard text is used by "Load from Clipboard" and paste actions in the editor. Diagnostics, error messages, and composition-skill materials are written to the clipboard when you copy them.

4. Export, sharing, and external links

Export and sharing start only through your explicit taps. The app shows the standard iOS share sheet and hands files to the app you choose (Mail, AirDrop, Files, various clouds, etc.). How recipients handle data follows each service's privacy policy.

When you open web links such as support pages, a connection to that site occurs. Inquiry emails reach the operator when you send them from your mail app.

5. Information received via inquiries

If you contact us by email, your sender address, message body, attachments, and any information they contain arrive at the operator's incoming mail server. The operator uses these only for replies, support, bug investigation, and necessary records — never for sales or advertising to third parties.

To review or delete past inquiry history, please contact our Privacy team. Please do not include confidential information or personal information unnecessary for your inquiry in emails.

6. This website and external services

This information site contains no analytics tools, ad tags, or tracking scripts that set cookies installed by the operator. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process source IP addresses and request logs for content delivery and infrastructure security. See GitHub Privacy Statement for details.

How the App Store, iOS diagnostic sharing settings, iCloud Backup, and other Apple platform services handle data is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

7. Children's information

The app has no feature that sends personal information to the operator. No personal information is collected even when children use the app. If a child's information is included in an inquiry, it is handled only as needed for support. Parents should avoid including unnecessary personal information in emails.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes due to app or site specification changes, legal revisions, etc., this page will be updated and the last-updated date at the top revised.
